Section 01

Executive
Summary

Backpack Air is a school-focused digital schoolbag designed for a future in which students use tablets instead of carrying a large collection of physical textbooks, notebooks and stationery throughout the school day.

Rather than attempting to replace a school's teaching system or Learning Management System, Backpack Air focuses on the student's everyday schoolbag.

The current prototype has been configured around Bahria College Karsaz, Class X-C to demonstrate how such a system could work in a real school environment.

It brings together
Authorized school textbooks
Digital notebooks
Timetable & exam schedule
Homework diary
Digital writing tools
Offline utilities

What does a student carry every day?
📚 Textbooks
📓 Notebooks

03
Section 03

What Backpack
Air Provides

One Digital Schoolbag — everything the student needs for the school day.

📖
Books

School-provided digital textbooks that can be available offline. Authorized by the school and publisher.

📓
Notebooks

Students can create subject notebooks with ruled, grid, dotted, graph, blank paper and custom covers.

📅
Timetable

The student's daily timetable is immediately available. Shows current class, next class, and full weekly view.

📋
Exams

Upcoming assessment information is presented directly in the schoolbag.

✅
Homework Diary

A personal place for students to keep track of schoolwork. Organized by today, tomorrow, upcoming and completed.

🛠️
Offline Tools

Calculator, formula book, sticky notes, focus timer, clock and other utilities — all work without internet.

The Question

Could the traditional school bag eventually become a digital one?

Backpack Air is an attempt to explore that question with a real school environment rather than simply building another education application.

We would like to demonstrate the prototype and discuss whether a small pilot could be explored with Bahria College Karsaz.
