Download Backpack Air.

No app store needed. Install it directly from your browser in seconds.

Scan to open backpackair.vercel.app

Android

  1. Open backpackair.vercel.app in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right)
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
  4. Tap "Install"
Backpack Air opens as its own app — full screen, no browser bar.

iOS / iPad

  1. Open backpackair.vercel.app in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (⬆ square)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add"
Runs as its own app on your home screen — full screen, no browser.

How to Sign In.

🎒

Student

  1. Open Backpack Air
  2. Enter your name
  3. Enter the school code from your class teacher
  4. Select your class
That's it — your digital schoolbag is ready.
👩‍🏫

Teacher

  1. Open Backpack Air
  2. Tap "I am a teacher"
  3. Select your school
  4. Enter your teacher ID and password
Your teacher dashboard loads instantly.
